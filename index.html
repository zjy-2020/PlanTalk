PlanTalk: Learning Future Motion Plans for Co-Speech Gesture Generation

Supplementary_material Code

Abstract

Co-speech gesture generation has made substantial progress in motion quality and speech--motion synchronization, yet existing methods predominantly map speech directly to motion or model semantic relevance at individual temporal positions. Such formulations provide limited explicit modeling of how a semantically relevant gesture should evolve over an upcoming temporal interval. We propose PlanTalk, a hierarchical speech-to-plan-to-motion framework that introduces a motion-grounded discrete plan as an intermediate representation between speech understanding and holistic motion realization. During training, a future motion-plan tokenizer encodes upcoming body-motion windows into discrete plan tokens, providing future-grounded supervision without requiring manually defined gesture categories or intention labels. A speech-to-plan predictor then learns to infer these plans solely from speech-semantic features, enabling future-plan prediction at inference without access to future motion. The predicted plans guide body-motion generation through two complementary mechanisms: an event-aware semantic gate refines when semantically relevant motion should be activated, while hierarchical plan-conditioned FiLM controls how the planned motion is realized across shared, body-part, and motion-token representations. Extensive experiments on BEAT2 and SHOW demonstrate that PlanTalk achieves state-of-the-art or competitive performance across motion quality, speech--motion synchronization, semantic relevance, and diversity, with particularly strong zero-shot generalization on SHOW.

Method

Overview of PlanTalk. Stage I learns a motion-grounded discrete future-plan vocabulary from offset future body-motion windows and freezes the pretrained tokenizer and codebook. In Stage II, a speech-to-plan predictor infers discrete plans from speech-semantic features using the frozen codebook, while the facial stream remains directly speech-conditioned. The predicted plans guide body-motion generation through an event-aware semantic gate for when semantic motion is activated and hierarchical plan-conditioned FiLM for how the planned motion is realized. Future motion is required only for plan supervision during training, not at inference.
